Dock Call

A phone tool for crew running a day of bump-ins around Perth. Parked at a stop, one tap: will I make the next dock window, and if not, why not?

⚠ Nothing here is real. Every time, route, incident and camera view below is invented to show the idea. No live data is connected, and the camera picture is a drawing, not a photograph.

What you’re looking at

Tap Ask about this run to see the whole flow: the driver speaks, the app answers out loud, and the answer ends with a decision rather than a map.

The point isn’t the ETA — Google already gives you that. It’s the four things underneath it: how much slack is left against the dock window, why it’s slow, what the camera actually shows, and what to do about it.

Choices made here

Screen
Always dark. It gets used at 4:30am in a truck cab, not at a desk.
Home view
The day’s run, not a search box. The driver already knows where they’re going.
Verdict
One word first — Clear, Tight or Late. Detail underneath for whoever wants it.
Spare time
The number that matters. Minutes against the dock booking, not minutes of driving.
Camera
Described in words as well as shown. A driver glancing at a phone shouldn’t have to interpret a picture.
Colour
Traffic-light greens, ambers and reds. Everyone reads them without being taught.

What it deliberately won’t do

  • Nothing while moving. Designed for use parked at a stop. Phones in hands while driving is illegal in WA, and this shouldn’t encourage it.
  • No background tracking. Closed or locked, a web app stops. It can’t follow anyone around.
  • No wake word. There’s no “hey app” on the web. It takes a tap to start.

Before this can be built

One question decides it: does Main Roads WA let us pull the camera images, and does the licence cover business use? If it doesn’t, the camera panel disappears and this becomes a much more ordinary app.

There’s a draft email sitting in the repo asking them exactly that.
